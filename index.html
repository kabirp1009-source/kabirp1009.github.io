TEDxRutgers — Budget & Expense Tracking

Role: Treasurer.

Work: Managed a $6,000+ annual budget across four teams. Oversaw more than 25 annual transactions and reimbursements, reconciled expenditures, and performed budget-to-actual variance analysis. Built accounts payable and receivable tracking systems that reduced reporting turnaround by three days.

Takeaway: Reliable records and clear communication help teams make informed spending decisions.

IRS VITA — Community Tax Preparation

Role: Volunteer tax preparer, January–April 2026.

Work: Managed the end-to-end filing process, reviewing W-2s, education expenses, and other financial records to determine filing status, taxable income, deductions, and credit eligibility. Supported more than 30 low-income individuals and families and helped clients collectively claim $45,000+ in credits and refunds while maintaining 100% accuracy through IRS quality review.

Takeaway: Accurate preparation and patient explanations build trust and make a meaningful difference for clients.

LIBOR — Stock Pitches & Valuation

Role: Member, Little Investment Bankers of Rutgers.

Work: Developed valuation knowledge through training in discounted cash flow (DCF), comparable companies, and precedent transactions. Delivered stock pitches on public companies, combining financial statements and industry analysis into investment recommendations. Evaluated macroeconomic trends and sector valuations across more than five industry deep-dives.

Takeaway: A defensible investment recommendation connects company performance, market conditions, and valuation assumptions.
